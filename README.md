#Exec Summary

      HEW University's Career Services Center serves more than 12,000 students but relies on static job bulletin boards and        manual resume reviews by a small team of career coaches. Team-Boogies will design and deliver a Talent Analytics & Job       Matching Platform that parses student profiles, course completion records, and project portfolios, then matches             students to corporate job postings using skill-matching algorithms. The platform will also show students the specific        skill gaps between their current profile and the jobs they want.

#Problem Summary

    - Low Matching Quality 
    - Static Job Posting 
    - Limited Coaching 
    - Skill Gap Visibility
