# Team-Boogies

## Project Charter: HEW Talent Analytics & Job Matching Platform

**Industry:** Higher Education & Workforce Development  
**Client:** HEW University - Career Services Center

## Project Overview
### Executive Summary
HEW University's Career Services Center supports more than 12,000 students but relies on static job boards and manual resume reviews. Team-Boogies will build a **Talent Analytics & Job Matching Platform** that reads student profiles, course records, and project portfolios, then matches students to employer job postings. Students will also see which skills they are missing for the jobs they want.

### Problem Summary
- Low Matching Quality: Employers receive candidates who do not fit their job requirements.
- Limited Coaching: A small team of career coaches cannot manually review 12,000+ resumes.
- Skill Gap Visibility: Students cannot see the skill gaps for technical jobs.
- Static Job Posting: Job postings are static and not personalized.

### Strategic Alignment & Benefits
| University Goal | How the Platform Helps |
|---|---|
| Better student career outcomes | Personalized job matches and skill-gap reports |
| Stronger employer partnerships | Better-qualified candidates |
| More efficient staff | Less time spent on manual resume reviews |
| Data-driven decisions | Dashboard showing in-demand skills and placement trends |

---

## Project Scope & Objectives

### Objectives
1. Load profiles for at least 90% of active students by the end of the pilot.
2. Reach a 75% employer-rated match quality score.
3. Cut coaches' manual resume review time by 40%.
4. Give every student a skill-gap report for each job they view.

### In Scope
- Student profile upload (resume, course records, portfolio links)
- Employer job posting portal
- Skill-matching algorithm
- Student skill-gap dashboard
- Admin dashboard for career coaches
- Connection to the university student records system and login

### Out of Scope
- Hiring, payroll, or onboarding for employers
- A separate mobile app (the website will work on phones)
- Alumni job placement
