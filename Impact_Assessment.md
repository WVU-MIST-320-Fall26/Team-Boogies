# Impact Assessment: HEW Talent Matching

## Requested Change
The Associate Dean wants to add live video interviews, automatic recordings, and AI-generated interview summaries to the platform.

## Project Impact
- **Scope:** Adding these features would expand the project beyond its original job-matching purpose.
- **Schedule:** Developing and testing these features could delay the project.
- **Cost:** Video calling, recording storage, and AI tools would require additional resources.
- **Risks:** Interview recordings could raise privacy concerns, and AI summaries could be inaccurate or biased.

## Team Recommendation: Defer
Our team recommends deferring this change because it would increase the project's cost, complexity and development time. We believe the team should focus on completing the original features before considering these additions in a future phase. Deferring the change would allow the team to focus on completing the original platform while giving time to evaluate the additional requirements and risks.
